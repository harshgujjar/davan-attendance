Photo Requirements

  • A recent passport-style photo — head and shoulders only.
  • Face the camera directly, eyes open, neutral expression.
  • Plain, light background — a wall works well.
  • Formal dress or college uniform.
  • Good, even lighting — no harsh shadow across the face.
  • Not a selfie taken at arm's length.
  • Not cropped out of a group, party or function photo.
  • No filters, beauty mode, stickers, caps or sunglasses.
This photo will be printed and projected at the Felicitation Ceremony. Please ask someone to take it for you against a plain wall — it takes a minute and it is what everyone will see on the day.
