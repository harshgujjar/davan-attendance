DISH 1

Chocolate Treat – Chocolate Peda

Soft, rich and melt-in-the-mouth.

✅ No fire

Ingredients

Milk powderCocoa powderCondensed milkGheeCardamom powderChocolate vermicelli / chips

How it's made

  1. Mix milk powder, cocoa powder and cardamom powder.
  2. Add condensed milk and a little ghee; mix into a soft dough.
  3. Shape into small round pedas.
  4. Decorate with chocolate vermicelli or chips.
🍩
DISH 2

Oreo Bread Donut

Crunchy Oreo outside, soft chocolate bite inside.

✅ No fire

Ingredients

Oreo biscuitsBread slicesMilkChocolate spreadPowdered sugarChocolate sauceSprinkles

How it's made

  1. Cut bread into rounds and make a hole in the middle to look like a donut.
  2. Dip lightly in milk and spread chocolate spread.
  3. Coat with crushed Oreo and powdered sugar.
  4. Drizzle chocolate sauce and add sprinkles.
🥃
DISH 3

Oreo Chocolate Dessert Shots

Creamy, chocolatey layers in a cup.

✅ No fire

Ingredients

Oreo biscuitsWhipping creamChocolate syrupChocolate chipsMilkChocolate shavings

How it's made

  1. Crush Oreo biscuits and mix with a little milk.
  2. Layer the crumbs in small glasses.
  3. Add whipped cream and chocolate syrup.
  4. Repeat the layers and top with chocolate chips and shavings.
🥣
DISH 4

Avalakki Bhel

Crunchy, tangy and spicy.

✅ No fire

Ingredients

Avalakki (poha)OnionTomatoGroundnutSaltChilliTurmericMixture

How it's made

  1. Take avalakki in a bowl.
  2. Add chopped onion, tomato and groundnuts.
  3. Add salt, chilli and a pinch of turmeric.
  4. Top with mixture and serve fresh.
