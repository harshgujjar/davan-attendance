💛 For the child

  • 🦁 A buddy animal they pick - greets them by name ("Hi Vihaan, ready to beat the 9 table?")
  • 🔥 Streak + 🏅 badges (Table Tamer, Spelling Star, Early Bird) - shown on home
  • 🎂 Birthday: confetti + "Happy birthday Vihaan!" from the app, extra stars
  • 🎵 Different happy sounds for right answers; never a harsh "wrong" sound
  • ⭐ Stars turn into a sticker book; 100 stars = a new buddy outfit
  • 🗣️ "I'm not sure I heard that" - never blames the child for the app's hearing

💌 For the parents

  • 📲 "Vihaan learnt the 9 table 🎉" note to Mom / Dad the moment it happens
  • 💌 Parent note read out at the next class ("Proud of you, beta")
  • 📅 Sunday summary: minutes, tables learnt, weak facts, best day
  • 📣 Call the child to class from office - the phone rings with their buddy
  • 🧓 Grandparents can be added to get the good news too

🧩 Families with 1 phone / 3 children

  • 1 phone, 1 child: child mode by default, parent part behind the PIN
  • 1 phone, 3 children: 3 cards, own times, back-to-back classes, "your turn in 2 minutes"
  • 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Family quiz: questions take turns between the children (optional)
All texts / badges / sounds live in data, so they can be changed without a new app.
